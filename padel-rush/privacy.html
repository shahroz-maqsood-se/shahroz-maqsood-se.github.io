Padel Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Padel Rush is a mobile game made by an independent developer. This page explains what information the game handles, why, and what happens to it. The short version: you can play as a guest, with your progress kept on your device and your Aces wallet on our game server under an anonymous ID, or sign in with Google or an email address to keep your progress in the cloud and appear on the rankings. The game uses a handful of Unity and Google services to run accounts, online matches, ads and purchases, and does not sell your data.

What we collect

You can play without an account. If you sign in, the game receives your email address (from Google or the email you type) to identify your account. You also choose a display name and a country, which are shown to other players in online matches and on the rankings.

Depending on the features you use, the services below receive some technical data from the app:

  • Player IDs — random identifiers created by Firebase Authentication (your guest or signed-in account) and by Unity Authentication (for online matches).
  • Game progress and rankings — if you sign in: your progress save, rating, weekly points, level, display name, country, friend code and friends list, stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Usage and crash data — which screens and modes are used, match results, and crash reports, collected by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve the game.
  • Advertising ID — the Android advertising identifier, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Purchase records — handled by Google Play Billing. The game receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded, not your payment details. Our game server checks each purchase with Google and keeps a record of it (product, purchase token, time) together with your Aces balance and the items you own.
  • Ranked results — for ranked matches, the game server records the match (players' IDs, result, time) to update ratings and to spot cheating; accounts with repeated cheating reports can be locked out of ranked play for a while.
  • Device and connection data — things like device model, OS version, IP address and app version, which the services below process to operate.

Where your progress lives

As a guest, your progress is stored on your device, and uninstalling the game removes it; your Aces wallet, owned items and ranked results are kept on our game server under an anonymous player ID, so purchases are never lost. When you sign in, a copy is kept in Google Cloud Firestore so you can continue on another phone; your display name, country, rating, weekly points and level are shown on the public rankings.

Services the game uses

Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay) — used only for online matches. Authentication gives you an anonymous player ID; Lobby finds an opponent; Relay carries match traffic between the two players. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — signs you in, stores your account's progress and the rankings, measures how the game is used and reports crashes. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google AdMob — shows advertisements in the game and uses the advertising ID, and approximate location derived from your IP address, to do so. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and Settings → Privacy choices lets you change it. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Padel Rush game server (runs on Cloudflare Workers, stores in Google Cloud Firestore) — holds your Aces, owned items and Pro Pass, checks purchases with Google Play, and records ranked results and ratings so they cannot be edited on the phone. It receives your player ID and the requests the game makes, plus your IP address as part of the connection. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases. Payment information goes to Google, not to us. See the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use it

  • To run online matches and show your display name to your opponent.
  • To keep your account's progress and show you, your friends and other players on the rankings.
  • To understand which parts of the game are played and to fix crashes.
  • To serve ads and process purchases that fund the game.
  • To keep the game working and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed above, who process it to deliver those features.

Children

Padel Rush is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Play offline: the single-player modes work without a network. When the phone is online, the game still sends usage statistics and crash reports and syncs your wallet.
  • Change or clear your display name at any time in the game's settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, change your ad consent in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Uninstall the game to remove all locally stored progress.

Delete your account and data

  • In the game: Profile → Delete account removes your account, cloud save, rankings entry, friends and your Aces wallet (Aces and owned items) straight away.
  • By email: write to the address below from the email on your account, or, as a guest, include the player code shown on your Profile. We delete the account and its data within 30 days.
  • What is kept: records of past purchases (product, purchase token, time) and of ranked matches are kept without your name, so a purchase cannot be claimed twice, refunds can be handled and cheating reports stay valid.

Player names and reports

Display names are public and must not be offensive, hateful, sexual or pose as staff. Any player can report a name from the rankings or after an online match; the report (your player ID, the reported player's ID and name, and when) is stored for review, and blocking hides that player's name on your phone. Names that break the rules are reset, and repeat offenders lose online play.

Keeping data safe and how long we keep it

  • Everything the game sends travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore security rules let each player read and write only their own data; wallets and purchase records can be changed only by our game server.
  • Account, progress and wallet data: kept until you delete your account.
  • Name reports: kept until reviewed, then deleted.
  • Purchase and ranked match records: kept to handle refunds, chargebacks and cheating, without your name.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: kept by Google under Firebase's retention settings, then deleted automatically.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
