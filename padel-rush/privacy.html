Padel Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Padel Rush is a mobile game made by an independent developer. This page explains what information the game handles, why, and what happens to it. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your device, uses a handful of Unity and Google services to run online matches, ads and purchases, and does not sell your data.

What we collect

Padel Rush does not ask for an account, an email address or your real name. The only thing you choose to give the game is a display name, which is shown to other players in online matches.

Depending on the features you use, the services below receive some technical data from the app:

  • Anonymous player ID — a random identifier created by Unity Authentication so that online matches work. It is not linked to an email, phone number or name.
  • Advertising ID — the Android advertising identifier, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Purchase records — handled by Google Play Billing. The game receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded, not your payment details.
  • Device and connection data — things like device model, OS version, IP address and app version, which the services below process to operate.

Where your progress lives

Your match history, unlocked gear, settings and ranking progress are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them. We do not keep a copy on our own servers.

Services the game uses

Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay) — used only for online matches. Authentication gives you an anonymous player ID; Lobby finds an opponent; Relay carries match traffic between the two players. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob — shows advertisements in the game and uses the advertising ID, and approximate location derived from your IP address, to do so. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown, and Settings → Privacy choices lets you change it. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases. Payment information goes to Google, not to us. See the Google Privacy Policy.

How we use it

  • To run online matches and show your display name to your opponent.
  • To serve ads and process purchases that fund the game.
  • To keep the game working and fix problems.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed above, who process it to deliver those features.

Children

Padel Rush is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Play offline: the single-player modes need no network and send nothing to the online services.
  • Change or clear your display name at any time in the game's settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, change your ad consent in the game under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Uninstall the game to remove all locally stored progress.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
